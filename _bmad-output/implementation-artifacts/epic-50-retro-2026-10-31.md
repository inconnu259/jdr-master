## Findings

### Behavior check (see section below)

- **F1 — dev API did not hot-reload host-edited files; both new routes answered 404.** *Fix now (environment).* Source: live check — `GET /parties/<id>/board-games` and `/host-counts` returned `Cannot GET …` (404) and both tabs showed « La requête n'a pas abouti. »; `docker compose logs api` showed the last start at 12:20 (before the 50.1 work), and the routes were mapped only after `docker compose restart api` (5:33 PM log lines). Automated tests (e2e) boot their own app, so they could not catch it. *Upstream lesson:* a story that adds a route must say « restart `api` » in its Verification, or the watcher must poll on a Windows bind mount.
- **F2 — the four group tabs do not fit at 360 px in any theme (acceptance criterion open since 50.1).** *Fix now.* Source: measured in the live app with Roboto 14px/500 and 24 px side padding per tab: natural widths **Grimoire 429 px, Forêt 370 px, Atelier 430 px**, against about 304 px of header width if the 28 px page gutters seen at 500 px apply, and against 360 px even with no gutter — so no theme fits; the Material tab header paginates. The browser window could not be set below 500 px, so a real 360 px render was not taken. Three tabs already overflowed in Grimoire (327 px) and Atelier (337 px); the new tab made Forêt overflow too (274 → 370 px). Recorded in `deferred-work.md` and in the 50.1 Implementation Notes. Epic AC: Story 50.1 « les quatre onglets tiennent à 360 px … mesure notée » (`epics-palier10-5.md:280`).
- **F3 — « chez Alice **,** moi » has a space before the comma.** *Fix now.* Source: screenshot of the « Joyaux » tab and DOM text `" chez Alice ,moi"` (`.ggt__owners`); cause is the whitespace text node inside the owner `<button>` in `apps/web/src/app/features/board-games/group-games-tab/group-games-tab.html` (owners loop), followed by the comma `<span>`.
